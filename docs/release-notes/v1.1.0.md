# oneMK 1.1.0: one picture on every screen, and big pictures

## What is new

- **Show the whole picture or video on each screen.** On the Wallpaper page, **On the screens** has two choices. **Across** is how it has always worked: one picture spread over all your screens, on every computer. **Each** shows the whole picture or video on every screen by itself, the same everywhere, and Fill, Fit and Stretch then apply to each screen on its own shape. Wallpapers you already set keep working as Across. A computer still on an older oneMK keeps spreading across until it is updated.
- **Big pictures work.** A photo of 80 or 100 megapixels (for example 13300 by 6226) was refused with "This picture cannot be opened", because decoding it needed more memory than oneMK allowed. oneMK now reads pictures up to 128 megapixels and keeps them at 8192 pixels on the longest side, which is more than two 4K screens side by side need. A picture over 128 megapixels now says "too large" instead of "damaged".

Nothing about pairing, sharing, updates or the service changed.
