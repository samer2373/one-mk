The brief

A fast, very light app that moves one mouse, keyboard, clipboard and files between the macOS, Windows and Linux computers on a desk, as if all the screens were one. Premium, precise, calm.

Final design

Pointer

A pointer cut by the edge of a screen: its tip has already crossed to the next computer and is lit.

  • The pointer is what the app moves; the cut is the screen edge it crosses
  • One shape, two colours: body and lit tip, readable from 16 px to a sign
  • Volt on deep green-black: no one in the category (all blues) owns it
