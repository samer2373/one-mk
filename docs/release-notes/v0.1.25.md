# oneMK 0.1.25: 4K videos keep their full size

## Fixed

- **A 4K video spread over wide screens is no longer shrunk first.** oneMK
  prepared a video at the size of the screens it covers, measured so the whole
  picture fits. With the default Fill, the picture is enlarged to cover the
  screens, so a 4K video on screens wider than 16:9 was made smaller (3840 to
  3412 pixels wide on a four-screen Mac) and then enlarged more, which looked
  softer. It now keeps its full size, and a video that needs no reduction is
  played as it is, without being re-encoded.

A 4K video is about 8.3 million pixels. Screens spread side by side can add up
to far more, and Fill crops the picture to cover them, so it still has to be
enlarged and will look soft on very wide setups. A video about 8000 pixels wide
(8K, or ultra-wide) stays sharp across them.

Choose the video again on the computer that prepares it (the Mac, if you add
videos there) to get the new copy.
