# one·mk 0.1.19: a new window, and setup without a terminal

## A redesigned window

- The Desk shows your computers on a 3D desk under frosted glass panels. Pick a
  computer in the sidebar to see its card: live round-trip time, which side it
  sits on, Switch to it, Send and Get.
- Keys & mouse shows a 3D keyboard and mouse. Click a key or a button to change
  what it does on your other computers.
- Status that needs your attention (broadcasting and how to stop it, another
  computer in control with Take back, a permission the desktop still needs)
  shows as a banner over the desk.
- Idle cost is the same as 0.1.18: about 0.7% of one core on the Desk with a
  live connection, and 1 to 4 MB more memory.

## No commands to set it up

- **The first time you open the window, it sets itself up.** one·mk starts when
  you log in, the file manager gets its "Send to" entries (Linux), and the
  background service starts. You don't need `one-mk install` any more. It runs
  once: if you later turn "Start at login" off, it stays off.
- On Linux, `install.sh` opens the window when it finishes.
- **macOS: a guide for the permissions.** When macOS needs Accessibility or
  Input Monitoring, one·mk opens the right System Settings page and puts a small
  card beside it, with an arrow at the list, the step you are on (1 of 2, 2 of 2),
  and a tick as soon as you turn the switch on. If you close System Settings too
  early, the card offers to open it again.
- **macOS: a designed installer window.** The disk image opens on a branded
  window with an arrow from one·mk to Applications and one line of instructions,
  readable in light and dark mode.

## Fixes

- "Start at login" from inside an AppImage now points at the AppImage file, not
  at its temporary mount, so it still works after you close the window.
- The brand colour tokens (`docs/brand/tokens.json`, `tokens.css`) are now
  written from the window's own style, so they can't fall out of date again.

## Not verified yet

- **macOS:** the permission guide and the installer window have only been
  compile-checked and drawn on Linux; they have never run on a Mac. In
  particular, the Accessibility list may show the entry as "one-mk" (the
  background service) rather than "one·mk". `docs/PERMISSION-GUIDE.md` and
  `docs/PACKAGING.md` have the checklists for a first run on a Mac.
- **Windows and macOS** in general are still compile-checked and unit-tested
  only. The new window has run on Linux (Intel and NVIDIA graphics), not yet on
  Windows or macOS, and not with a screen reader.
- **There is still no live wallpaper.** It stays experimental and off by default.
- **Automatic updates** stay off until an update signing key is set (see
  `docs/UPDATES.md`).
