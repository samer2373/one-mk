What we heard

The brief

A free, open-source tool that shares one mouse and keyboard across macOS, Windows and Linux computers. It should feel fast, precise, calm and quietly confident, and show what it does at a glance.

fastprecisecalmtrustworthy

Readable as 'one pointer moving between computers' at 16 px, works in one colour and reversed, amber as the only accent.

Final design

Crossing

A laptop and a monitor with one amber pointer on the seam between them: one mouse and keyboard, crossing to the next computer.

  • Says what the app does in one glance, at any size
  • Pixel-fitted 16, 22 and 24 px drawings keep both computers in the tray
  • Amber appears only on the pointer, the same signal the app uses for live state
