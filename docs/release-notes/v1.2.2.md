# oneMK 1.2.2: the pointer follows your hand at once when it comes back to a Mac

## What is new

- **Mac: no pause when the pointer comes home.** When the pointer came back from another computer, it showed at the edge of the Mac's screen but did not follow the mouse or trackpad for about a quarter of a second (macOS ignores the mouse for that long after the pointer is moved by a program). The pause is now switched off. Measured on a real Mac with hand-driven crossings between a Mac and a Linux laptop: the pointer first followed the hand after a median of 254 ms before, and after 14 ms with this change. If you still feel a longer hang when coming back, please tell us how long it lasts.
- **Optional anonymous analytics, off by default.** **Settings → General → Privacy** has a new switch, and `one-mk analytics on | off | status | reset` does the same from a terminal. Nothing is collected unless you turn it on, and even then nothing is sent unless the program was also given a PostHog project key and host (no key is built into this release). When on, it shares which features worked or failed and redacted error reports under a random installation ID, never what you type, your clipboard, your files or paths, or the other computers' names. Turning it off deletes the ID and anything waiting to be sent. Details: [docs/analytics.md](../analytics.md).

Nothing about pairing, sharing, the service, the update signing key or the update format changed.
