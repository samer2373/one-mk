The idea

A laptop and a monitor, and one pointer standing on the seam between them. That is the whole product: one mouse and keyboard, crossing from one computer to the next. The pointer is the only amber on the page, and a clear gap separates it from the devices.

Masters: logo/onemk-symbol-*.svg, logo/onemk-lockup-*.svg, logo/onemk-wordmark-*.svg. The clearance around the pointer is a real hole, so the mark works on any ground.
