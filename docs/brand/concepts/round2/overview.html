Recommendation

01 Twin. It is the most ownable and the most legible at 16 px: a real letter from the name, built to Geist proportions, where the amber stem is the single shared input standing between two screens. It scales into a system: the amber pillar can light up per connected machine in the tray, and the m sits naturally in front of the wordmark.

Shared system

Wordmark set in Geist SemiBold from the real outlines, tracked tight, with the interpunct as a deliberate amber pixel centred on the x-height. Ground #0F1216 · ink #EDEFF2 · signal #FFB547. Tray icons use the bare mark; the app icon puts it on a dark squircle.
