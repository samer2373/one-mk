# oneMK 0.1.23: sharper video wallpapers

## Fixed

- **Video wallpapers no longer lose a lot of quality.** A video larger than
  your screen (a 4K file on a 1080p monitor, for instance) is prepared once at
  the screen's exact size, and that copy was written with a very low bitrate
  (about 2.8 Mbps for 1080p), so detail turned blocky. It now uses about
  2.7 times more (about 7.5 Mbps for 1080p30, 30 Mbps for 4K30) and never more
  than the original had.
- **Videos that already fit your screen are no longer re-encoded.** A video at
  or below your screen's size and at most 60 frames per second used to be
  rewritten whenever its bitrate was above a low limit, which only cost
  quality. It is now played as it is, unless its bitrate is extreme (more than
  four times the target). Playback cost follows size and frame rate, so it
  stays the same.

Choose the video again to get the new copy; the old prepared one is cached.
