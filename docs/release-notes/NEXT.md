# Unreleased: a new look

Notes for the next release, kept here until its version is chosen (then this file becomes
`v<version>.md`). The window and the brand are new; pairing, networking and the service work exactly
as before.

## A new look

- **Light and dark.** The window comes in a light and a dark look. **Settings → General → Appearance**
  (or "Appearance: Light/Dark/System" in the palette, or `one-mk appearance light|dark|system`) picks one;
  System, the default, follows your computer as it changes, and switching cross-fades. The 3D desk,
  keyboard and mouse stand on the page's own floor in both.
- **Tabs along the top.** The sidebar and the narrow icon rail are gone. The pages are tabs in a bar along
  the top (Desk, Arrange, Wallpaper, Clipboard & files, Keys & mouse, Health, Settings), with the status,
  Search (`Ctrl+K`, `⌘K` on a Mac) and the one main button at the right. Arrange is a page of its own, so
  the page shortcuts are `Ctrl+1` to `Ctrl+7` (`Ctrl+,` still opens Settings). An update waiting shows as
  "Update ready" in the bar; **Stop** is in Settings → General, the palette and the tray.
- **One panel beside the 3D desk.** Choosing a computer describes it in a panel at the right: how fast it
  answers, Switch to it, Send files, Get clipboard, where it sits and its own settings. In a small window
  the panel moves under the desk.
- **Calmer pages.** Arrange draws the screens' edges as a flat plan (drag, arrow keys or an exact X, Y);
  Wallpaper slices the picture across your real screens; Health opens with one line saying whether all is
  well; Settings shows one category at a time under a row of pills.
- **Status is never colour alone.** ● online, ◆ needs you, ○ off, ■ error, everywhere.
- **New type:** Inter for the interface and JetBrains Mono for numbers, keys and commands.
- **New brand.** The oneMK symbol is now a keycap with the pointer on its top, in cobalt. The app icon,
  the tray icons (four states, for light and dark menu bars), the installer artwork and the documents use
  it. The old green-and-black pointer is retired.
