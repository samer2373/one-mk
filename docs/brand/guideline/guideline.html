oneMK brand guideline

The Pointer · November 2026 · CLI one-mk

The mark

A pointer cut by the edge of a screen. Its tip has already crossed to the next computer and is lit: one mouse and keyboard, on every screen on the desk.

edges 15° and 60° · notch 165° and 90° · cut at 135°, 6.5/256 wide · Geist wordmark

Colour versions

Dark grounds: reversed. Light grounds: colour (the tip keeps an ink edge). On the accent and on photos: one colour.

Small sizes

Below 32 px use the pixel-fitted drawings: 16 px (16–20), 22 px (21–23), 24 px (24–31). They keep a whole-pixel cut so the tip still reads.

Clear space and minimum size

Clear space on every side: the cap height of “MK” (in the symbol alone, a third of its height). Minimum: symbol 16 px; horizontal lockup 18 px tall (90 px wide, 20 mm in print).

Wordmark

Geist (SIL OFL), drawn as outlines: “one” at weight 600, “MK” as small capitals at 88 % height and weight 660 so the stems match. Always “oneMK”; the CLI is one-mk.

App icon

Deep green-black glass tile; frosted white body; the lit tip. 16–32 px use a flat, pixel-fitted drawing.

Tray states (16 / 22 px, true size and 16 px ×4)

Off: hollow, dim

Idle: tip unlit

Connected: tip lit

Controlling: the pointer has crossed

Distinct in one colour: off is hollow, idle has a dim tip, connected lights the tip, controlling leaves only the tip solid.

Don't

Don't recolour the tip

Don't pull the tip away

Don't rotate or tilt

No glows or gradients on the flat mark
