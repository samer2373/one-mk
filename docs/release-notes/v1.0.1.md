# oneMK 1.0.1: About, and who makes it

A small release on top of 1.0.0.

## What is new

- **An About card in Settings → General.** It shows the oneMK logo, the version, the licence, and who makes it: **Bigfoot Technology** (Amman, Jordan), with a link to https://bigfootjo.com/. It also carries the credits (the 3D mouse model, CC BY 4.0, and the fonts). **About oneMK** in the Ctrl+K palette opens it, and the other Settings tabs end with a quiet "oneMK 1.0.1 · About" link.
- **The tray menu has "Made by Bigfoot Technology"**, which opens the site; the macOS About panel says it too.
- **The Bigfoot logo** is part of the app now; it belongs to Bigfoot Technology and is not under oneMK's licence (see `docs/brand/bigfoot/`).

Nothing about pairing, sharing, updates or the service changed.
